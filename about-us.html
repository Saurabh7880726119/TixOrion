WHO WE ARE

Making exams simple, secure and stress-free

ExamPro is an online examination platform built for the way Indian institutions actually work. From a single class test to a large recruitment drive with thousands of candidates, we give you the tools to create, conduct and evaluate exams from one place.

We started with a simple observation: paper-based exams take too much time, cost too much, and leave little room for insight. Our platform removes the manual work, so teachers and administrators can focus on teaching and decision-making instead of paperwork.

  • Create exams in minutes with MCQ, descriptive or mixed questions
  • Secure proctoring with anti-cheating controls
  • Instant results and detailed performance reports
