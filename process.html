How we build your product

Six steps, each with clear deliverables, so you always know what happens next.

1

Discovery & planning

We learn your goals, audience and requirements through workshops and technical analysis, then agree on scope, timeline and success metrics.

2

Design & prototype

We create wireframes, UI designs and interactive prototypes for you to validate before development starts. Your feedback is built in at every stage.

3

Development

Agile sprints with regular standups, code reviews and continuous integration. You can see progress throughout the build.

4

Testing & QA

Unit, integration and user acceptance testing, plus performance and security checks, so the product is stable before it goes live.

5

Deployment

A smooth CI/CD release with monitoring set up from day one. We handle everything from staging to production.

6

Support & growth

After launch we provide maintenance, performance monitoring and steady improvements, so the product grows with your business.

What makes our process different

The practices behind every project we deliver.

Agile methodology

  • 2-week sprint cycles with clear deliverables
  • Daily standups to track progress
  • Sprint retrospectives for continuous improvement
  • Flexible scope as your needs change
  • Regular demos so you can give feedback early

Quality assurance

  • Automated unit and integration test suites
  • Manual testing by dedicated testers
  • Load testing on all key user flows
  • Security vulnerability scanning before launch
  • Cross-browser and cross-device checks

Transparent reporting

  • Project dashboard access for clients
  • Weekly progress reports and milestone updates
  • Dedicated Slack or Teams channel
  • Issue tracking with clear resolution timelines
  • Monthly strategy reviews on long-term projects

Security first

  • OWASP security guidelines followed throughout
  • Data encrypted at rest and in transit
  • Role-based access control by default
  • Penetration testing before production launch
  • Compliance requirements considered from the start
