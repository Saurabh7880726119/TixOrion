How ExamPro Works

From setting up your institution to reading the final report, here is how simple online exams can be.

Home  /  How It Works
3 SIMPLE STEPS

Create, conduct and analyze

Everything happens in one platform, so you never have to switch between tools or handle papers.

1

Create

Set up your organization and prepare your exams. You can start with a single class test and grow from there.

  • Set up your organization and add your logo
  • Add admins, teachers and students with the right roles
  • Build exams with MCQ, descriptive or mixed questions
  • Save questions in the question bank to reuse later
  • Set timing, marks, instructions and schedule
2

Conduct

Candidates log in and take the exam securely from anywhere, on the device they have.

  • Candidates sign in with their own login
  • Exams run on desktop, tablet or mobile
  • Anti-cheating measures protect the exam
  • Admins can watch the exam with real-time monitoring
  • Answers are saved and submitted securely
3

Analyze

As soon as the exam ends, results and reports are ready, so you can act on them straight away.

  • Automatic evaluation with instant results
  • Detailed reports for each student and batch
  • Analytics that show strengths and weak areas
  • Review answers and share results with ease

TWO POINTS OF VIEW

What it looks like for each user

For institutions

  1. Register your institute and add your branding.
  2. Add users such as teachers, staff and students.
  3. Create the exam from your question bank or new questions.
  4. Monitor live while students are taking the exam.
  5. Download reports and act on the insights.

For candidates

  1. Receive your login and exam schedule from your institution.
  2. Sign in on a laptop, tablet or phone at the exam time.
  3. Read the instructions and start the exam.
  4. Answer and submit within the given time.
  5. See your result as soon as your institution releases it.
